About us

Kerad builds technology for a more private internet.

We believe privacy shouldn’t be a premium feature. Modern software routinely trades user data for profits.

In the age of growing mass surveillance powered by AI, Kerad seeks to be its foil — we apply machine learning to the benefit of the user’s freedom and privacy, not against it.

Our work has a focus on systems that reduce reliance on centralized surveillance. We’re a small, independent team based in the United States.

What we’re working on

Kerad’s current projects explore decentralized and privacy-preserving alternatives to the infrastructure most of the internet depends on today.

By using our software, you support the greater mission of a free and open internet.
